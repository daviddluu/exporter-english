Three real listings, before and after

Each "before" is copied from a live Made-in-China.com listing. Supplier names are withheld. Facts the original didn't give are left for the factory to confirm, never invented.

Pillow packing machine (flow wrapper)

Packaging · Liaoning · US$9k–25k
Before

Biscuits / Cakes / Medicine / Daily Necessities / Industrial Parts / Cartons / Chocolate / Bread / Noddle Pillow Packing Machine

"This machine is used for packing the product that are not suitable the brush to feed, like chocolate, waffle, puffing, nougat etc."

After

Horizontal Flow Wrapper GS-350 | Servo Pillow Pack Machine for Chocolate, Wafers & Snack Bars | 40–220 Packs/Min

Wrap chocolate, wafers, nougat and snack bars at up to 220 packs per minute. The GS-350 is a servo-driven flow wrapper for products that can't go through a brush feeder, and it links straight to your line.

What changed
  • The title had nine unrelated products. Now it leads with the terms buyers search ("flow wrapper"), plus the model number and speed.
  • The listing gave two different speeds (60–220 and 40–220). That's flagged for the factory to confirm.
  • Features rewritten as buyer gains: no empty bags, size changes on the touchscreen, no stops for film changes.
  • Added a buyer FAQ that answers product size, film types and line connection before anyone has to message.

Industrial meat and vegetable dicer

Food processing · Shandong · US$3k–5k
Before

Vegetable Frozen Industrial Automatic Pork Meat Cube Cutting Chopping Processing Dicer Machine

"…we focus on the R&D, design, production and sales of enema production lines, vacuum packaging machines…"

After

QDJ-350 Industrial Meat Dicer | Cubes Fresh & Frozen Meat, Vegetables and Fruit | SUS304, Hydraulic or CNC

"…we've shipped sausage production lines, dicers, tumblers, vegetable washers and fryers to buyers in North America, Southeast Asia and the Middle East."

What changed
  • "Enema" was a literal translation of 灌肠 (sausage stuffing). To a Western buyer it means a medical device, so it's now "sausage production lines".
  • The page named two different companies. Now it uses one name throughout.
  • Fixed a spec typo (a 190 mm machine length that should be 1940 mm) plus "Forover" and "Datails".
  • Flagged the facts buyers need that were missing: output in kg/h, cube size range, and Hydraulic vs CNC.

Multihead weigher vertical packing machine

Packaging · Jiangxi · US$10k–15k
Before

Banana Chips/ Shrimpchips/Nut Automatic Solid Snack Vertical Packing Machine

"It can automatically complete bag making, measuring, blanking, sealing… and can be equipped with cursor, coding, inflation…"

After

Multihead Weigher VFFS Packing Machine SD-S420 | Snack, Chips & Nut Bagging | 0.5–1 kg Bags, Mitsubishi PLC

Forms the bag, weighs, fills, seals, cuts and counts in one cycle. Options include date coding, nitrogen flushing and a registration-mark photo-eye for printed film.

What changed
  • Translated the machine-manual terms into what buyers say: "combination scale" became multihead weigher, "cursor" became registration mark, "blanking" became product discharge.
  • Added "multihead weigher" and "VFFS" to the title. Those are the two phrases buyers type into search.
  • Kept the strongest part, the name-brand parts list and priced spares, and moved it up where buyers will see it.
  • Flagged a founding date that conflicted (2012 vs 2017) before a buyer notices it.
